What is Online Alkansya?

This Online Alkansya is a simple personal money tracker designed especially for students. It helps you keep track of your allowance, income, expenses, and savings in one place. This is not a real banking or payment system—it's a digital tool where you can manually record and monitor your money, helping you understand your spending habits and manage your budget wisely.

Track income and expenses

Record your allowance, extra income, and daily expenses. Organize your transactions into categories like Food, School Supplies, Bills, and Transportation.

Savings goals

Set a savings target for something you want to buy and monitor your progress as you record your savings.

Monitor your budget

Record your allowance and part-time income, then see your total, how many times you received it, and the last one.

Weekly budget

Set a budget for the week and see how much is left each day. You also can’t spend more than the money you recorded.

See where your money goes

A spending breakdown by category shows which expenses take the biggest share of your money.

Your own account

Sign up to get your own profile and settings. Change your password and set your own limits. Each user sees only their own records.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I add my allowance?

      Go to the Dashboard, choose Income, type a description and the amount, pick Allowance as the category, then press Add income.

      Why can’t I add an expense?

      You can only spend the money you have in the tracker. Add income first. Money you put into savings goals is not available to spend.

      How do budgets work?

      Open Budgets to set a monthly limit for each expense category. The weekly budget is on the Dashboard.

      How do I buy something from a savings goal?

      Open Savings Goals and add savings until you reach the price. Then press Mark as bought. The saved money is recorded as an expense.

      Where is my data saved?

      Only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data or use another device, your records won’t be there.

      I forgot my password.

      On the Log in page, press Forgot password? Then enter your email and the full name on the account.

      For tracking purposes only

      No real money is stored, sent or managed.
