About Online Alkansya

Making Personal Money Management Simpler.

A smarter and more organized way to understand your money, manage your allowance, and build better financial habits.

What is Online Alkansya?

Online Alkansya is a web-based personal finance tracking application designed especially for students and individuals who want a simple way to manage their personal finances. It provides a convenient digital space where users can manually record their income, expenses, budgets, and savings goals.

Instead of relying on handwritten notes or scattered records, Online Alkansya brings personal financial information together in one organized platform. It helps users understand where their money goes, monitor their spending patterns, and become more mindful of their financial decisions.

Why Online Alkansya?

Managing money can be challenging, especially for students who depend on allowances or have limited budgets. Small daily expenses can easily add up without proper tracking.

Online Alkansya was created to make personal financial tracking easier and more accessible. Its goal is to encourage financial awareness, responsible spending habits, and realistic savings goals.

Who Is It For?

Online Alkansya is suitable for students, young adults, and individuals who want to organize their personal financial records. Whether tracking a daily allowance, monthly income, everyday expenses, or personal savings goals, the application provides a simple way to keep everything organized.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Online Alkansya free to use?

Yes. Online Alkansya is free to use. You only need to create an account with your name, email address and a password.

Does Online Alkansya handle real money?

No. It is a personal finance tracking tool, not a bank or a payment service. All amounts are typed in by you, and the app does not store, transfer or process actual money. Your records are saved only in your own browser.

Do I need a bank account?

No. You do not need a bank account, card or e-wallet. You simply enter your own allowance, income and expenses to keep track of them.

Can I edit or delete transactions?

Yes. Open the Transactions page to edit or delete any entry, and use the search box and the Income and Expenses filters to find it quickly.

Can I monitor my monthly expenses?

Yes. Reports & Analytics shows a summary for any month, a spending breakdown by category and a 6-month trend, and the Budgets page lets you set monthly limits for each category.

Can I create savings goals?

Yes. Add a goal with the price of what you want to buy, then put savings into it step by step. Each contribution is listed, and you can mark the goal as bought when you reach the price.

      Quick answers about using Online Alkansya

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I add my allowance?

      Go to the Dashboard, choose Income, type a description and the amount, pick Allowance as the category, then press Add income.

      Why can’t I add an expense?

      You can only spend the money you have in the tracker. Add income first. Money you put into savings goals is not available to spend.

      How do budgets work?

      Open Budgets to set a monthly limit for each expense category. The weekly budget is on the Dashboard.

      How do I buy something from a savings goal?

      Open Savings Goals and add savings until you reach the price. Then press Mark as bought. The saved money is recorded as an expense.

      Where is my data saved?

      Only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data or use another device, your records won’t be there.

      I forgot my password.

      On the Log in page, press Forgot password? Then enter your email and the full name on the account.

      For tracking purposes only

      No real money is stored, sent or managed.

      Every amount is typed in by you. Online Alkansya is a budgeting and tracking tool, not a bank or payment system.
